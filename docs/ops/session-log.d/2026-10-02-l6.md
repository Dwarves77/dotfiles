## A genuine mechanism gap found and resolved, not silently worked around

The brief frames the watcher as reusing "the exact pattern spec 08 S6 already shipped for
carbon_intensity_tkm" through `methods/index.ts`'s registry, invoked by drain.ts's existing recompute
pass. Read `drain.ts` in full (306 lines, as required): its Pass 2/2b dispatch is hard-coded to stale
`derived_values` rows (by `method_id`/`method_version`) and stale `inference_records` rows - `signposts`
is neither table, and `PK_COLUMN`'s allowlist (the only tables a `MethodFn` can receive as resolved
`ctx.inputs`) does not include `signposts` or `research_assessments`. A `MethodFn` is also contractually
pure (no `sb`, per `methods/index.ts`'s own header), so it structurally cannot write the
`propagation_events` row or the assessment transition rule 17 requires. Wiring `signposts` into drain.ts's
literal dispatch loop would mean adding it to `PK_COLUMN` and a third Pass 2c loop - exactly "touching
drain.ts beyond registering a method," which the brief forbids.

Resolution: `computeSignpostWatch` is registered in the method registry exactly as asked (satisfies the
seam convention, and gives a future lane that DOES wire `signposts` into drain.ts's dispatch a real,
tested method to call). The actual firing-with-writes path is `fireSignpost`, a sibling export with its
own `sb`-shaped contract - the same separation `register-derivation.ts` already has from `drain.ts`
(pure write-shape vs. the loop deciding when to call it). This is not a contradiction of the ruling this
lane was told not to reopen (the entity-FK/spine ruling); it is a mechanism-level gap the brief's prose
did not fully resolve, closed here per rule 13 and named in full rather than silently assumed.
