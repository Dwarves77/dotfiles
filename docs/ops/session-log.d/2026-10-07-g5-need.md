## Decisions

- A type with no authority floor (market_signal, initiative, regional_data) needs only a rated source to count as
  a holding; the standard own-authoring-body floor of 4 (migration 202) is not applied, so a standard needs a
  floor-2 holding to close its need (stricter, named in the module header).
- The tier compared is `tier_override ?? base_tier` (the credibility skill: dynamic `effective_tier` never confers
  reg-fact eligibility).
- A term-need flag uses `subject_type: system`, `subject_ref` the vocabulary term id; a lineage need uses
  `lineage:<identifier>` and created_by `term-need:standard`.
- CELEX ids raise no flag: they are explicit register targets (counted in the summary, listed in `celex_targets`).
  No Federal Register branch exists because the lineage detector produces no Federal Register shape.
