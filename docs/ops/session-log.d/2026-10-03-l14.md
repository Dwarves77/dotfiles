## PPWR numbers, read from the Regulation text (CLAUDE.md rule 2) [CONFIRMED]

Source: Regulation (EU) 2025/40 of 19 December 2024 on packaging and packaging waste, EUR-Lex
`CELEX:32025R0040` HTML, fetched 2026-10-03 and read directly (the DG ENV summary page does not carry
the numbers, per spec 04 section 8).

- Art. 7(1), from 1 January 2030 or 3 years after the Art. 7(8) implementing act, whichever is later,
  minimum recycled content in any plastic part of packaging, average per plant and year: (a) 30 percent
  contact-sensitive PET packaging except single-use beverage bottles; (b) 10 percent contact-sensitive
  non-PET plastic except single-use beverage bottles; (c) 30 percent single-use plastic beverage bottles;
  (d) 35 percent other plastic packaging.
- Art. 7(2), from 1 January 2040: (a) 50 percent; (b) 25 percent; (c) 65 percent; (d) 65 percent.
- Art. 7 covers plastic only. Art. 7(15): by 12 February 2032 the Commission reviews other materials.
  Exemptions: Art. 7(4), 7(5). Adjustment or derogation by delegated act: Art. 7(12), 7(13).
- Art. 24(1): maximum empty space ratio 50 percent for grouped, transport and e-commerce packaging, from
  1 January 2030 or 3 years after the Art. 24(2) implementing acts, whichever is later.
- Art. 29(1): at least 40 percent of transport packaging (pallets, boxes, crates, IBCs, drums and so on)
  reusable within a re-use system from 1 January 2030; endeavour 70 percent from 1 January 2040.
  Art. 29(5): grouped packaging in boxes, 10 percent from 2030, endeavour 25 percent from 2040.
  Art. 29(6): beverages by final distributors, 10 percent from 2030, endeavour 40 percent from 2040.

Only Art. 7 numbers are used in code (`materials-ppwr-join.ts`, cited per row). The empty-space and
reuse numbers are recorded here for spec 04 section 8's open confirmation; no code uses them.

## Built

- `fsi-app/src/components/operations/FeasibilityGateStrip.tsx` (new): the gate strip (one wrapping line,
  headline plus five labelled chips) and `MaterialsPpwrRows` (a collapsed native `details`). Gate classes:
  PPWR thresholds, EPR registration and authorised representative, PFAS limits, national permitting,
  ETS2. The gate type has no numeric member; a `@ts-expect-error` proof in the file fails `tsc` if a
  numeric score field is ever accepted. Headline order: blocked, incomplete (any unassessed class),
  conditional, clear; a region with any unassessed class never headlines clear. No gate count is shown.
- `fsi-app/src/lib/operations/materials-ppwr-join.ts` (new): pure join over the matrix's existing
  `materials_sourcing` facts. The caller supplies `materialKey` from a closed union (the four Art. 7
  categories plus `non_plastic`); there is no label parsing. One threshold per row, article cited.
- Tests: `materials-ppwr-join.test.mjs` 7 of 7, `FeasibilityGateStrip.npmtest.mjs` 6 of 6.
- `fsi-app/.discipline/rendering/smoke/feasibility-gate-strip-smoke.mjs` (new), registered in
  `ux-smoke-specs.mjs`; F35 `ROW_COMPONENTS` line added for the strip (coordinator ruling replaced "the
  coordinator adds it").
- `fsi-app/src/components/operations/RegionDimensionMatrix.tsx` (edited): an always-rendered block of
  fixed height `FEASIBILITY_BLOCK_HEIGHT` (168px, `overflowY: auto`) above the 300px panel slot, which is

  depends on selection (operator criterion A).

fixed height. No baseline entry was added.

## UX compliance

**Feasibility block (FeasibilityGateStrip plus MaterialsPpwrRows, mounted above the matrix panel)**:
primary goal is seeing, before any cost figure, whether a region is eligible (spec 04 section 1). Path:
zero clicks, the block sits above the panel and shows when a region is selected; one tap opens the PPWR
threshold rows. One primary action: none, the strip is read-only (the `details` summary is a secondary
disclosure, 44px minimum height). Feedback states: none asynchronous, the block renders synchronously from
props. Empty state: with nothing selected or in compare mode it says "Select a region to see its
feasibility gates". Every unassessed gate shows the one `Absence` token, never "clear". The block has a
fixed height so the card does not move under the reader (law on stable layout; operator criterion A);
overflow scrolls inside it. The title carries `data-guard-title` via the shared `SectionHeading`.
