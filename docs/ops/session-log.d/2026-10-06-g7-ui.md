## UX compliance
- Screen: Corrections panel (`/admin/items/[id]`). Goal: fix a wrong value on one item and know it took effect. Path: open the item (tab search or link), press the action beside the value, say why, save (three steps). One primary action per block: its Save. Feedback: save shows "Saving..." and disables the form; success closes the form and shows a status line that says customers see it now and how to undo it; failure shows the API's own message and keeps the input. Revoke: first press opens a confirmation that says the machine value will be restored, confirm shows "Revoking...", success shows a status line, failure keeps the reason. Targets are 44 px or more (smoke measured at 375 and 1280).
- Screen: Corrections tab (admin dashboard). Goal: see every correction and act on the ones that need attention. Path: open the tab, filter, open the item or revoke in the row (two steps). One primary action: Revoke and restore (inside its confirmation). Feedback: loading line, read error with Try again, empty state that says what to do next, filter-empty state, revoke as above.
