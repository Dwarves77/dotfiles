## 5. WHERE TO INVEST OR NOT

Each entry states the evidence on when it pays, when to wait, and the conditions — never a recommendation to a specific business, per the task's binding rule. "Economics not sourced" is stated wherever true rather than estimated.

**Primary shipment-level ISO 14083 emissions data capture — invest now, conditionally.** Pays because CountEmissionsEU is EU-anchored and client demand (luxury Scope 3, OEM tenders) already exists ahead of the 2030 deadline; also seeds France L1431-3 compliance. [CONFIRMED transport.ec.europa.eu]

**SAF/low-carbon-fuel "green" product resale to clients — invest only where a client has a funded Scope-3 commitment.** BCG: average willingness-to-pay for low-carbon shipping fell 4.5%→3% (lowest since 2022), no one pays above 20%, refusal rate up 4 pts; Scope-3-committed buyers average 5% WTP vs ~1% without. TIACA: only 30% of air-cargo respondents see positive financial return from sustainability spend (down 12 pts) despite SAF deployment rising to 40%. Avoid broad-market "green surcharge" rollout; target narrowly. [CONFIRMED bcg.com, stattimes.com]

**Reusable/pooled crate systems (fine art) — invest where return flows exist, avoid otherwise.** Pooling/rental pays on repeatable round-trip lanes (touring circuits, fair circuits, museum loan loops); the documented failure mode is one-way transit (Christie's case under the point-to-point ROKBOX model). ## 7. What this means for the product — proposal, not adopted

Read per instruction: `docs/specs/07-page-walkthrough.md` and `docs/specs/00-foundation-the-spine.md`.

**Where the existing intent already covers the four questions, if fully built:**
- **Q1 (what's happening)** maps directly onto **Regulations** for regulation-shaped families (its position-split, obligation card, deadline rail and horizon lane in spec 07 are essentially this report's §2 and §4, built as a live product surface) and onto **Research**'s horizon bands / assessment card for the non-regulatory, forward-looking families (F8's insurer resistance, F13's survey trends) — *if* Research is rebuilt away from its current doctrined misrouting as an editorial queue.
- **Q2 (how it affects me)** maps onto **Market Intel**'s carbon-overlay-on-corridor and lead-time-chart concepts (spec 07 §Market Intel) — "of €3,650, €96/FEU is EU ETS, trending +18% YoY" is precisely this report's Family 1 framing, rendered per-shipment instead of per-family narrative.
- **Q3 (compliance duty)** maps onto Regulations' obligation card fields (owner, due date, evidence-to-keep, Yes/No/Not-assessed position) almost one-to-one with this report's §4 calendar.
- **Q4 (invest or not)** maps onto **Operations**' automate-vs-hire and feasibility-gate model (break-even wage, NPV, feasibility gates evaluated before cost) — this is the only part of the existing spec intent built specifically to answer "where to invest," and it is also the surface both this report's holdings check and the spec's own §Operations text describe as furthest from built ("stub gallery... no real content for most jurisdictions").

**Where the existing intent is too narrow, as a proposal for the operator:**
1. **The corridor/item view (spec 00 §6, spec 07 "how the five fit together") assumes every family reduces to one corridor and one regulation.** Several of this Map's highest-scoring families do not: F5 (green-claims regulation) is a marketing-copy and B2C-claims question with no corridor at all; F13 (survey evidence) is meta-evidence about pricing assumptions, not a corridor fact; F9's cargo-theft/fraud family is a carrier-vetting and security-investment question that touches every corridor at once rather than being anchored to one. The five-lenses-on-one-spine architecture may need a sixth object — a **policy/claims-compliance lens** that is not corridor-shaped — or an explicit acknowledgment that some families are legitimately cross-corridor and the UI needs a "no single corridor" render path rather than treating that as a data gap.
2. **Operations' comparison-matrix model (spec 07 §Operations) is regional/facility-siting shaped.** Several of this report's §5 invest-or-not calls are not facility decisions at all — they are fleet-purchase-timing decisions (zero-emission trucks), insurance-product decisions (GA cover), and certification/tooling decisions (DG training, TAPA certification, CBAM-filing capability). The spec's automate-vs-hire and region-comparison framing does not obviously extend to "should I buy this certification" or "should I buy this insurance product" — that may be a real product gap distinct from Operations' current build backlog.
3. **The Community surface (spec 07 §Community) is the only place spec 07 explicitly designs for the kind of aggregate, anonymised benchmark this report's §5 SAF-pricing and green-premium-WTP evidence would ideally feed** (e.g. "This quarter: what SAF premium are you seeing on EU–US air?"). None of the Map's survey evidence (BCG, TIACA, EcoVadis) currently has a stated path into Community's house-benchmark mechanism; that linkage is not addressed in either read spec document and is worth an explicit decision.
4. **The Assistant guardrails (spec 00 §7) correctly forbid it from rendering a compliance verdict** ("are we compliant with FuelEU?" returns the obligation and the user's own data, never a verdict) — this is consistent with this report's own refusal to give investment recommendations rather than conditional evidence, and is worth the operator noting as an intentional design parallel, not a gap.

This is a proposal only; no scope change is adopted by this report.

## 8. Survey evidence on forwarders' own priorities

All entries `[CONFIRMED]` per the Map's Family 13 table, carried here without added interpretation.

- **Descartes 9th Forwarder/Broker Benchmark** (n=434, published 4 Nov 2025, with AfA/AICBA/BIFA/CIFFA/CLECAT/FIATA/NCBFAA): top 5-year challenges are customer pricing pressure (61%), global instability (60%), tariff changes (42%). "Carbon taxes/credits" was added as a new answer option in 2025; the percentage itself is not extractable from the chart. 65% expect AI to deliver most IT value in the next 2 years.
- **BIFA Business Leaders Forum** (n=434, 1 Oct 2025): Customs Broker as an offered service fell from 70.5% (2023) to 58% (2025); FTZ/bonded-warehouse services rose from 8.09% to 13.6%. Sustainability appears grouped qualitatively under "Uncertainty/Regulation/Talent" with no numeric weighting extractable.
- **TIACA 6th Air Cargo Sustainability Survey** (n=81, fielded Nov 2025–Apr 2026): external sustainability pressure fell to 53% from 61% (lowest since 2021); regulatory pressure down 19 points over two years; only 30% perceive positive financial return (down 12 points) despite SAF deployment rising to 40% (up 8 points). Sample shrank sharply from the prior wave (n=274) — the Map itself flags year-on-year comparisons as "directionally suggestive but statistically fragile."
- **TIACA 5th wave** (n=274, 20 Mar 2025): 71% had a sustainability strategy (84% large firms vs 60% small); 42% had a dedicated budget; 35% used offsets.
- **BCG Low-Carbon Shipping Demand survey** (n=125 cargo-owner decision-makers, fielded late 2025, published 30 Apr 2026): average willingness-to-pay for low-carbon shipping fell from 4.5% to 3% (lowest since 2022); no one pays above 20%; Scope-3-committed buyers average 5% WTP vs ~1% without. Covers ocean freight specifically, not air or forwarders.
- **EcoVadis Sustainability Ratings Index** (10th ed., ~200,000 scorecards, 2021–2025): 78% of suppliers lack science-based targets; 74% have no upstream Scope 3 reporting; 30% provide zero carbon data at all; 68% of buyers now use AI tools for sustainable-procurement screening.
- **WREC Coalition Green Procurement 3Ws mapping** (humanitarian sector, surveyed Jul–Aug 2025, published 26 May 2026): supplier-evaluation criteria are the most-implemented green-procurement lever (27%), ahead of tendering criteria (24%) and supplier engagement (21%).
- **GCC/AAM modal-shift progress data — a finding of absence, not a survey result:** no public progress data exists anywhere against the Gallery Climate Coalition's 2028 non-air-majority target. Five years into a voluntary sector campaign with no measurement of attainment is itself evidence, and is treated as such in this report's §2 and §3 (fine art).
