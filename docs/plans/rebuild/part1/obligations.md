## 8. Build plan for this module

### What the module is

An **obligation** is what a regulation requires of whom by when. Today the system holds it at two grains. The event grain (`item_forward_events`, migration 274): one dated statement extracted verbatim from an item's grounded text, with an event kind (entry_into_force, compliance_deadline, review_or_report, phase_step, consultation_close, other), a precision (day, month, year), the clause around the date, and a confidence (high when the source is a FACT or GAP claim span, medium when it is rendered section markdown). The register grain (`obligations`, migration 290): one row per forward event, carrying the item's jurisdiction codes, leg modes and a `binding_position` (direct_duty, carrier_passthrough, customer_contract, monitoring_only, or NULL for not yet classified) so the Regulations register filters and sorts without a join. The object grain spec 01 section 3.2 asks for (`obligation_objects`, migration 376: pinpoint citation, verbatim text, duty holders, applicability trigger, four dates, cost slots, status Yes/No/Not assessed) exists as a table and a writer with no input. **Forward events** are the timeline: the Upcoming strip, the rail card, the Market policy timeline and the Dashboard's Due next all read dates that this module extracted or synced.

### Derivation rules the code embodies (the rules a port must keep)

1. A date becomes an event only after a trigger phrase (extract-forward-events.mjs RULES 641 to 792: 18 rules in four kind families plus two Portuguese phase rules) is followed by a parseable date (DATE_TRY_ORDER 100 to 105: ISO, full EN, full PT, month-year; bare years only for `by`, `from`, `as of`, `since`, never when followed by `/`, 136 to 145). The emitted `source_span` is the date text alone, verbatim in the source (assertVerbatim 151 to 160), weekday prefixes skipped (111 to 116).
2. Kind is never guessed: a bare-year `by` or `from` with no deontic (`shall`, `must`, `is required to`, `is due`, `should be submitted`, 637 to 638) or aim word (639) in the sentence is a skip with a reason (947 to 953); `as of` and `since` are candidates promoted only when a deontic clause follows (1015 to 1075); a `for <segment>` tail or transitional-period language reclassifies to phase_step (956 to 958); `By <date>, ... report` wins over the generic deadline (686 to 693 before 703 to 711); `shall apply from X to Y` and `from X until Y` emit the window end as `other` (982 to 1007).
3. A date equal to the run date or the brief's `last_regenerated_at` date with no commencement verb in its clause is refused (815 to 836, 966; read-and-extract.mjs 25 to 49 supplies both dates); a status-only clause (`in force as of`, `updated as of`) is refused regardless (821, 1058).
4. The display text is the clause around the date, bounded (300 before, 160 after), opened at a genuine sentence, paragraph, list or `[slot_key]` boundary, cleaned of table cells, bold labels, FACT/GAP labels, citation keys and URLs, and marked with an ellipsis when it is a fragment (248 to 292, 398 to 471); a record-facts FACT wrapper is unwrapped to the quoted passage containing the date, a GAP wrapper is skipped (482 to 562).
5. A `[due_date]` slot claim (record-facts.mjs 542 to 561) whose span alone yields no event is rescued by re-scanning the capture context around the span (1158 to 1166, 1352 to 1407), with three named skip reasons; the finer of the extractor's and the slot's precision wins, never quarter (1101, 1131 to 1135).
6. Within one item, two hits with the same date and kind whose texts are the same sentence after comparison normalisation collapse to one, claim-backed kept over section-backed, every drop recorded (1185 to 1279).
7. A section-backed event survives only if its date span is verbatim in a FACT claim span of the same item (read-and-extract.mjs 233 to 268, the D10 class fix); claim-backed events are verbatim by construction.
8. Register rows copy the event's kind and date; `due_date` and `date_precision` are null together; modes are leg modes only (never multimodal); status is archived when the item is (derive-obligations.mjs 111 to 131). binding_position is decided in a fixed order: the instrument's identity (legal instrument, short name, instrument_identifier, canonical_instrument_key) against the spec 01 table; else the record-facts `[binding_position]` claim; else the title with generic phrases accepted only where the rule allows (CBAM for an EU item, never PPWR); else monitoring_only when every known duty-holder class excludes the forwarder role; else NULL (79 to 94; classify-binding-position.mjs 119 to 162).
9. Derivation is idempotent on forward_event_id (filterNewRows 158 to 161 plus the UNIQUE of 290) and writes fresh rows only, never a mutation.
10. compliance_deadline is the earliest `event_date >= today` among the item's compliance_deadline events, ties broken by confidence (high first) then id; an existing value is never nulled (compliance-deadline-sync.mjs 12 to 24, 47).
11. Customer reads join only verified, non-archived items and drop a row whose item fails the join (read-register.mjs 298 to 299, 475 to 476, 485; read-upcoming.mjs 218 to 219, 167). The register's order is three segments: upcoming soonest first, past most recent first, undated last by id (380 to 384); every filter is applied in the query and the total is the sum of three exact counts (436 to 450). The upcoming read defaults to every kind but `other` (read-upcoming.mjs 10), from today, and scopes by workspace jurisdictions and modes or by the URL facets (98 to 113).
12. An obligation object is written only from a candidate whose verbatim text is in its named capture of the same instrument item, with known duty-holder classes, a well-formed trigger, status not_assessed, version prior plus one with supersedes set (derive-obligation-objects.mjs 41 to 119); the DB trigger of migration 376 re-proves the span.
