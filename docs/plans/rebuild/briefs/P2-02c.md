THE DEFECT [CONFIRMED 2026-10-16, parity run 38941331372 on main 8b2ee7b8, next-start.log]: signed in as a member, every page
died. /api/workspace/bootstrap (P2-02) calls serviceClient() unconditionally; on the parity runner there is no service key, so the
route threw "SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY is not configured" 608 times; the identity and bootstrap loaders (P2-01 identity-loader,
P2-09 use-bootstrap) retried without a bound, 10,000 requests in minutes, the per-IP limiter answered 429 in a storm, and no page
ever settled (screenshot timeouts). Two causes, both design: (1) a customer's own pages depend on the service role to render;
(2) a client retry with no ceiling.

WHY THE FIX IS DESIGN, NOT A KEY IN CI: WHY-2026-10-16 section 4 ("rules enforced where the data lives": RLS) and least privilege.
A member's pages read the member's own org rows, which RLS already grants to the member's client (org_memberships, profiles of
org mates through the existing policies, workspace_item_overrides, item_* workspace tables, workspace_settings). The service
role belongs to writes a policy cannot express, to the admin console behind its gate, and to the machine. The parity job stays
WITHOUT a service key on purpose: from now it is the mechanism that proves a member's site renders under RLS alone.
