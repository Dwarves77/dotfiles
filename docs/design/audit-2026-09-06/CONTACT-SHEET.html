NOT CAPTURED — real gaps, not oversights
  • Mobile 390px and tablet 1024px — see the panel above; the tooling could not set the viewport.
  • Logged-out flow: /login, /signup, /onboarding, /workspace/new, invitation accept. The session is authenticated so these redirect.
  • Loading states — first contentful paint measured at 14.1s with 44 chained fetches; nobody has looked at what renders during it.
  • Overlays: Export brief, Share, the per-row ⋯ priority dropdown, the Ask AI panel.
  • Empty-result states: a search or filter combination returning nothing.
  • Content extremes: most captured items sit at the sparse end; a data-rich regulation and market signal are not represented.
  • Roles: everything seen as an admin owner. Never as a plain member, a read-only viewer, or a logged-out visitor.
