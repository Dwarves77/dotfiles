0The system — every shared part, defined once
0.1 · Resolutions
Each audit finding → one system rule
§4 · 5 vocabulariesOne urgency scale, four words — Immediate · Action · Monitor · Awareness — each with a fixed time definition. Market's "Cost alert / Window closing / Competitive edge" become a separate signal-kind tag.
§3.1–3.2 · 2 token setsOne set. Red · orange · blue · green — four hues a reader can name. The grey Moderate/Low scale and the amber/yellow pair are deleted. No additive migration.
§3.3 · 2 accentsInk is the accent. Buttons, active nav and rules are #5A5552; text is #1A1A1A. Pure black never appears as a fill. Orange is released to mean Action only. Every colour on a page means urgency or severity — nothing else.
§5.1 · 2 nav widthsPanels open with a 3px rule in the accent (#5A5552) (band-coloured on a band block) and sit on the paper with one shadow: 0 1px 2px at 4% and 0 4px 14px at 6% ink. Rows inside a panel never carry their own. One frame: 220px nav card in a 16px gutter · content 1180px max · right rail 300px. Identical on list, detail, admin and account. Nothing shifts on navigation. The dashboard title is {first name}'s brief, bound to the signed-in user.
§5.2/§22 · 5 detail shapesOne detail architecture for all surfaces: header → sticky section index → sectioned document → right rail. Tabs are gone; sections are anchors, so nothing is hidden behind a click.
§5.3/§23 · click targetsThe whole row is the link, 56px minimum, on every list. Secondary controls (watch, ⋯) live in a 44px end column behind a rule.
§6 · NOT SCORED rowsMeters replace words. Every row carries a 4-bar micro-meter — the detail page's four impact dimensions at glance size. Unscored = hollow bar, never a sentence.
§5.6 · repeated ask barOne command bar in the masthead of every page; search and Ask share it. No per-tab ask panels — ~140px returned to content on every detail view.
§8 · buried factsFact cards. Every extracted fact renders as claim / operative date / source tier / link — never as FACT: … *Source: inside a paragraph. Inference and Legal-confirmation are labelled variants of the same card.
§7/§13 · one-page partsTimeline and meters on every surface. Full versions in the detail header and rail; micro versions on rows, tiles and the dashboard. They appear wherever an item does.
§9 · absence statesOne quiet convention. Absence is a muted small-caps reason, inline, in place of the value. Never a bordered box, never red. Only a gap the reader must act on gets a card.
§21 · dead controlTwo depths, not three. Summary and Full brief. "Complete brief" is removed. Any control drawn here is wired or it is not drawn. Overflow rule: any fixed cell (exposure, at-a-glance, table cell) clamps to 3 lines and grows an accordion "More ▾" that expands in place — never a tooltip, never truncation without a way in.

S1Summary
Generated · 30-second read
The EU ETS extension to maritime transport, enacted through Directive (EU) 2023/959 of 10 May 2023, entered into force on 5 June 2023 and applies from 1 January 2024 to CO₂ emissions from ships of 5,000 gross tonnage or more calling at or departing EEA ports. Shipping companies surrendered their first allowances by 30 September 2025 for 2024 emissions; the 70% tranche for 2025 emissions falls due 30 September 2026, and 100% from 2027.
Who is obligated
Shipping company / vessel operator — not the forwarder
How it reaches you
Carrier ETS surcharge on any EEA-touching ocean lane
What to do by Sep 30
Request line-item ETS disclosure; model the 70%→100% step in 2027 budgets
