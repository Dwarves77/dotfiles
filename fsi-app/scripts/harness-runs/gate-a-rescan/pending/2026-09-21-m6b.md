## Change

Registered the `gate-a-rescan` harness family by descriptor (`family.json`, `FAMILY.md`), closing the
loop-manifest's `brief-apply-to-gate-a-rescan` and `population-turn-to-gate-a-rescan` hops (build plan
section 6.1 row M6, docs/dispatches/lane-briefs/2026-09-20/brief-m6-amendment-1.md sections C and D).
`.github/workflows/gate-a-rescan.yml` gains `workflow_run` triggers on "Brief apply" and "Population
turn" completing, plus a `workflow_dispatch` (`mode`, `limit`, `loop_run_id`), and a final step that
builds and commits this family's own `gate-a-rescan-run-NNN.json` artifact (config: trigger, mode, limit,
upstream_name, upstream_run_id, loop_run_id, scope_source, attach; per_item: one row per re-scanned item)
via the new `scripts/turns/emit-gate-a-rescan-artifact.mjs`. Before this lane no re-scan existed at all --
`item_gate_a_state` only ever got written at mint time or by `scripts/mint/heal-provenance.mjs`'s own
per-item pass.
